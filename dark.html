About Me

QA Engineer in Progress

Hello! I am an IT undergraduate at Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka, pursuing a BSc (Hons) in Computing and Information Systems. I am passionate about Quality Assurance and Software Testing, and I enjoy analyzing software, identifying issues, and ensuring applications provide reliable and user-friendly experiences.

I am currently developing my skills in manual testing, test case design, test execution, defect reporting, and test documentation. I also have experience with automation testing using Java, Playwright, Selenium, JUnit, and Maven.

I enjoy finding software defects and making sure applications work as expected from a user's perspective. I am continuously improving my knowledge of QA processes, API testing, automation testing, and CI/CD practices. My goal is to build a career as a QA Engineer and contribute to delivering reliable, high-quality software.
